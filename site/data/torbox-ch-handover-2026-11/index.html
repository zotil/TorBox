TorBox

TorBox is an easy-to-use, anonymising router based on Raspberry Pi, which also runs on Debian, DietPi and Ubuntu-based systems. TorBox can create a separate WiFi that routes the encrypted network data over the Tor network. However, TorBox also supports clients who are connected to a cable. As TorBox mini, it can be plugged into a USB socket and be used as a network device which sends all the traffic to the Tor network. TorBox on a Cloud can be used on a cloud combined with an OpenVPN server — it is literally a private VPN with tor functionality. Locally installed or on a cloud, TorBox also helps to publish data easily and safely through Onion Services. The type of client (desktop, laptop, tablet, mobile, etc.) and the operating system on the client don't matter.

Maintainer handover

After many years of developing and maintaining TorBox, I have decided to hand the project over to Zotil, who will continue its development from now on.

The decision didn't come overnight. Over time, my motivation for the project faded, and my interests shifted towards artificial intelligence, especially local language models. I want to put my time and energy into that field. TorBox deserves a maintainer who can give it the attention it needs.

I couldn't imagine a better successor. Zotil has been contributing to TorBox for more than six years and is responsible for its Python components, such as the excellent TorBox Wireless Manager. He knows the project inside out, and I am convinced that it is in the best of hands with him.

I would like to thank everyone who used, tested, and supported TorBox over the years, and especially Zotil for taking over. Please direct all questions, bug reports, and contributions to him from now on.

Zotil, I wish you every success with TorBox!

Verifying the handover

For cryptographically verifiable confirmation that this handover is legitimate, download the handover notice and its PGP signature. The TorBox key lives in the repository as PUBLICKEY.asc, so this stays verifiable even after the old website is retired:

# 1) get the TorBox key (in the repo, not on the retired site)
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zotil/TorBox/master/PUBLICKEY.asc | gpg --import
# 2) verify
gpg --verify handover.txt.asc handover.txt
# expect: "Good signature from ... TorBox <anonym@torbox.ch>"
# (a "not certified"/untrusted note is normal)

Archived copies of both keys are also on this page: torbox-public.asc (former maintainer, 69E1…E840) and zotil-signed.asc (Zotil, cross-signed by the TorBox key).
